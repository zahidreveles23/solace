# Build in public: week 1 posts

Post once a day on X (Twitter), plus LinkedIn or Threads if you use them. Short, honest and specific beats polished.
Attach a screenshot when you can. Always use real numbers, even small ones; people follow the journey.

---

**Day 1: the start**
> Starting a challenge: build a real business from $0 with AI and share every number publicly.
>
> The product: a $19/mo tool that helps local businesses get more Google reviews. Podium charges $300+ for this.
>
> Goal: 10 paying customers in 30 days. Day 1 revenue: $0. Let's go.

**Day 2: why this idea**
> Why Google reviews?
>
> I looked up barbershops near me. The one with 180 reviews is booked for days; the one with 23 reviews, 2 blocks away, is just as good.
>
> Most happy customers never leave a review because nobody asks at the right moment. That's the whole business.

**Day 3: the product** (attach a screenshot of the review page)
> The product is live.
>
> Business owners text customers a one-tap link, or put a QR code on the counter. Customers land right on the Google review form.
>
> Built with Claude Code in one session. Now the hard part: selling it.

**Tips**
- Reply to everyone who comments. That's how an audience grows early.
- Don't fake numbers. Honest $0 posts often get more support than wins.
- Every Friday, post the numbers. Consistency matters more than anything else.
